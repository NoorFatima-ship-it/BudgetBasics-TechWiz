LEARN WITHOUT JUDGEMENT

Money habits to watch for.

Everyone makes money mistakes. Spotting a pattern is how you start changing it.

01Impulse buyingPurchasing in the heat of the moment+

Student scenario: You see a flash sale and buy shoes you had not planned for.

Why it matters: Unplanned purchases can crowd out costs you care about more.

Try this: Pause for 24 hours and ask whether you still want it tomorrow.

02Ignoring small expensesLittle spends can quietly add up+

Student scenario: Daily snacks seem small, but surprise you at month's end.

Why it matters: You may lose track of money available for your goals.

Try this: Notice a few everyday purchases and set a comfortable weekly limit.

03Late paymentsForgetting a bill or a due date+

Student scenario: A shared utility contribution slips your mind during exams.

Why it matters: It can cause avoidable stress and strain shared arrangements.

Try this: Add a reminder to a calendar you already use.

04Unused subscriptionsPaying for something you rarely use+

Student scenario: A free trial turns into a monthly charge you forgot about.

Why it matters: Recurring costs can be easy to overlook in a monthly plan.

Try this: Review subscriptions every month and cancel ones you no longer use.

05Spending without a planDeciding where money goes after it's gone+

Student scenario: You spend freely at the start of the month and run short later.

Why it matters: Without a rough plan, it is harder to protect money for essentials.

Try this: Give your income a simple plan before the month begins.
